Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

PassLane ("the app") is published by Sanchit Jangir. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We do not collect your data

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information on our servers. We do not run a server for the app, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

  • Your study progress (answers, scores, streaks, your chosen test and test date) is saved only on your device. It is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS. We never see them.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether you have an active purchase.

Children

The app is suitable for teenagers preparing for a learner's permit. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the new version at this address.
